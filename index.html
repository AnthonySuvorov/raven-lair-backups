Raven Lair Backups

Backup service for the Raven Lair Discord community.

Raven Lair Backups is a private backup utility used to protect the database and operational records of the Raven Lair Discord bot.

The service uses the Google Drive API to upload and manage backup files in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner.

This is a private utility. It is not a public file-sharing service and does not provide access to users' Google Drive files to other people.
